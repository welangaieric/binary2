Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get the code?

To get a code, scroll through our packages and click on select. Enter your phone number, you will be prompted to enter your mpesa pin. After this, you will be connected automatically.

Where can I get my code if I already purchased it?

Voucher codes are normally sent to you via sms. If you didn't get one after your purchase, click on self care on top of the page to access it.

How many devices can I connect?

The number of devices you can connect is only one.

Is the WiFi secure?

No, this is an open and public WiFi. You are advised to safeguard your personal information while connected to it.

My connection is slow. What can I do?

If you're experiencing slow speeds, try moving closer to an access point, restart your device. Peak usage times (evening) may affect performance.
